Lightning

Lightning within a 10 mile radius stops all games at that location with one long blast of a horn. Everyone leaves the fields immediately and waits in a vehicle or off site.

A full 30 minutes must pass with no strikes before the all clear. Every new strike resets the 30 minutes. The all clear is a horn blast. Do not return to the fields until you hear it.

Delays

A game that has passed halftime is called final after 60 minutes of delay. If a delay runs past 60 minutes, any game already at or beyond halftime is final at the time it stopped.

Coaches game

There is no coaches game in 2026. Field availability did not work out this year. It returns in 2027, and the referees have already been warned.

Scores are posted by the match referee and confirmed by tournament staff.
